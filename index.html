Midlife care that meets you where you are

After. Harbor is clinician-guided metabolic care—not a prescription mill. We focus on eligibility, honest history-taking, and a paced plan. If medication is part of your care, it is only after a licensed clinician reviews your information and decides it is appropriate.

Adults exploring clinician-guided metabolic and lifestyle support in midlife. Not for everyone. Final eligibility rests with your clinician.

About medications we may discuss

Some members may be candidates for FDA-approved therapies when clinically appropriate and available. In limited, individualized situations, a clinician may consider a compounded medication from a licensed pharmacy.

  • Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The FDA does not review them for safety, effectiveness, or quality before they are marketed. They are not generics.
  • We do not claim compounded products are the same as—or the same active ingredient as—any brand-name drug (including Ozempic®, Wegovy®, Mounjaro®, or Zepbound®).
  • If prescribed, dispensing is by a licensed partner pharmacy (not After. Harbor as manufacturer).
  • Your clinician decides. You can decline medication and still discuss non-drug support where offered.

What to expect

Change looks different for everyone. We do not guarantee a specific amount of weight loss or any particular outcome. Nutrition, movement, sleep, and follow-up adherence matter. This is not a cure for obesity, diabetes, or any disease—medication, when used, may be one part of a broader care plan.

Individual results vary. Not everyone is prescribed medication.

Safety & risks

Common side effects can include nausea and other digestive changes. Serious risks exist—discuss them with your clinician before starting any medication. Tell your clinician if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have a history of thyroid cancer (including MTC), MEN2, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, or severe stomach-emptying problems; medication may not be appropriate.

This website does not provide medical advice. Clinical advice comes from your licensed clinician. Seek urgent care for severe or unexpected symptoms.

Questions we hear most

Is this Ozempic / Wegovy / Mounjaro / Zepbound?

No. Those are separate FDA-approved brand-name products. If medication is appropriate, your clinician discusses options. Compounded products are not FDA-approved and we do not claim they are the same as any brand-name medicine.

Am I guaranteed medication?

No. A licensed clinician reviews your information and may decline. Paying for evaluation or membership does not guarantee a prescription or shipment.

How fast will I lose weight?

We don’t promise timelines or amounts. Individual results vary. Lifestyle and follow-up matter.

Who prescribes?

A licensed clinician licensed in your state—not an algorithm alone. After. Harbor is doctor-founded midlife care, not a questionnaire-only mill.

Who compounds or dispenses?

If a compounded medication is prescribed, a licensed partner pharmacy prepares and dispenses it. After. Harbor does not manufacture these medications.

When am I charged?

Nothing is charged before a clinician approves your plan. If approved and you continue, membership is $199/mo all-in on this cash-pay path.

Can I cancel?

Yes — cancel anytime* on this cash-pay path. Cancel-anytime and charge-timing language must match live checkout.

Do I need insurance?

No — this is a cash-pay membership path. We do not bill insurance for this program on this page.
