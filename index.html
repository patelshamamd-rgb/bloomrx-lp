Questions we hear most

What is compounded semaglutide / tirzepatide?

Prepared by a licensed U.S. compounding pharmacy when prescribed for you. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not the same as FDA-approved brand-name products. Your clinician decides if compounding is appropriate.

Is this the same as Ozempic / Wegovy / Zepbound?

No. We do not claim sameness or equivalence to any brand-name drug. This page is for compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide only, if prescribed.

How much does it cost?

$199/mo all-in on this cash-pay path — medication (if prescribed), clinician access, and shipping as described. Price stays flat as dose is paced. Confirm HSA/FSA with your plan administrator. Cash-pay GLP-1 in market often lands ~$150–400/mo; $199 is the locked all-in price.

When am I charged?

Nothing is charged before a clinician approves your plan. If approved, charge then ship.

How fast does the process move?

Quiz ~2 minutes → clinician review typically within 24 hours → shipping after approval. How your body responds is individual.

What about side effects?

Nausea, digestive changes, and other effects can occur. Your clinician reviews risks and contraindications. This page is not medical advice.

Do I need insurance?

No — this is a cash-pay path designed for people whose coverage denied, dropped, or stalled obesity medication. We do not bill insurance for GLP-1 on this page.
